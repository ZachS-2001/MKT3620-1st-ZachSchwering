Fort Dodge Senior High

I grew up in Fort Dodge, Iowa, and attended Fort Dodge Senior High. That’s where my interest in how things are built and how systems work started, and it’s a big part of why I first set my sights on engineering when I got to college.

Iowa State University

I enrolled at Iowa State University as an engineering student. I spent a few years fully invested in that major — enough to build a real technical foundation and to genuinely test whether it was the right fit. What I found was that I liked the problem-solving and the technical rigor, but the parts of engineering that energized me most were the moments that involved communicating with people: explaining a plan, coordinating a team, translating something technical into terms a client could act on.

That realization led me to switch into Marketing. I’m now a senior, graduating in the spring of 2027, and the switch has turned out to be less of a detour than a sharpened focus. I still carry an engineer’s instinct for structure and precision into my marketing coursework, and I’ve built on it with a growing skill set in communication, strategy, and understanding what an audience actually needs to hear.

The combination is the point: a marketing background paired with real engineering coursework and years of hands-on technical field work gives me a perspective that a lot of marketers don’t have — I can sit in a technical conversation and keep up, and then turn around and explain it clearly to someone who has never set foot on a jobsite.
