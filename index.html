Hi, I’m Zach Schwering.

I’m a senior in Marketing at Iowa State University, graduating in the spring of 2027. I grew up in Fort Dodge, Iowa, and came to Iowa State originally to study engineering. A few years into that path I realized my strengths were better suited to marketing, and I made the switch — but the technical foundation I built along the way never left. It shows up in how I work today: organized, detail-driven, and comfortable moving between hands-on technical settings and customer-facing conversations.

For the past three years I’ve interned with McClure Engineering, where I’ve worked across on-site surveying, in-house technical projects, and jobsite assistant management. Alongside that, years of customer-facing retail work sharpened my ability to listen closely, ask the right questions, and match people with the solution that actually fits their needs.
